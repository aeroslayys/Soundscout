## Discover places beyond the pictures

A café can look peaceful and still be packed and noisy. A library might be calm in the morning but busy by evening. Photos and ordinary reviews rarely tell you what a place actually sounds like.

**SoundScout helps you discover places through their sound.**

Whether you're looking for somewhere quiet to study, a relaxed spot to work, or a lively place to meet friends, SoundScout gives you a clearer idea of the atmosphere before you visit.

## Find your kind of atmosphere

- **Explore an interactive map** to discover places around you and in areas you're planning to visit.
- **Compare community sound ratings** to see whether a place is loud, lively, moderate, calm, or silent.
- **Check different times of day** with morning, afternoon, evening, and night sound trends.
- **Find quieter places nearby** with Quiet Near Me.
- **See sound patterns on the map** with the sound heatmap.
- **Filter by accessibility** to find places with wheelchair or step-free access and gender-neutral toilets.
- **Check recent updates** before deciding where to go.

## Real experiences from the community

SoundScout grows through the people who use it.

Visited somewhere recently? Share how it sounded, add useful accessibility details, and help others make a more informed choice. You can also suggest a place that isn't on the map yet.

Your contributions help build a picture of how places feel throughout the day—not just how they look.

## More context with optional sound analysis

When submitting a rating, you can optionally provide a short ambient recording for sound-level analysis. This adds another point of reference alongside the community's experience.

Sound measurements are reported in dBFS, a digital recording level—not a calibrated professional sound-pressure measurement. Results can vary between devices and recording conditions, so they are best treated as supporting context rather than an absolute measure.

**Your recordings are not kept.** Audio is temporarily processed for analysis and removed afterwards; SoundScout retains derived measurements, not the recording itself.

## For places and the people who run them

SoundScout also gives participating venues a way to connect with their visitors.

Venue partners can:
- View recent community ratings and sound trends for their venue.
- Keep visitors informed with event announcements.
- Share event details such as timings, expected crowd levels, and whether amplified music is planned.

Venue partner access is invitation-based. Interested venues can contact the SoundScout team to learn more.

## Made for wherever you go

Use SoundScout on desktop or mobile. The web experience is responsive and can be installed on supported phones as a Progressive Web App, so discovering places feels at home on the device you already use.

## Get started

4. Sign in when you're ready to rate a place or contribute a venue.
